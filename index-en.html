KiwiSleepy

White Noise, Routines, and the best sleep for your baby

Designed by parents for parents. KiwiSleepy combines relaxing sounds, structured routines, and detailed tracking to help you understand and improve your little one's sleep cycles.

Create the perfect environment for rest and establish healthy habits from day one.

Everything you need for their nights

Essential tools to ease sleep and bring peace of mind to the whole family.

🎵 Relaxing Sounds and White Noise

A wide library of high-quality sounds: from classic white noise and nature to traditional lullabies. Includes a special Red Mode, timer with progressive fading, and the ability to record your own voice.

⌚ Total Control from your Wrist: Control volume, stop, or activate sound without touching your phone, directly from your smartwatch or wearable.

🕒 Customized Routines

Establish consistent habits that regulate your baby's biological clock. Set custom bedtime steps and receive reminders that guide your little one's sleep.

🔔 Follow routines effortlessly: Get all notifications on your smartwatch too, so you don't miss a step and can follow the routine comfortably while caring for your baby.

📊 Tracking and Sleep Diary

Keep detailed track of naps and nighttime sleep. Log wakeups, waking mood, and feeding details. Visualize history to identify patterns and optimize your little one's rest.

🍼 Our Story as Parents

Hello! We are the creators of KiwiSleepy and, above all, we are parents. Our adventure began with the birth of our baby. Like many of you, we went through those endless nights where exhaustion mixed with worry.

We tried everything, from white noise to strict routines, and realized that having all these tools in one place made our lives easier. We decided to create KiwiSleepy to share with other parents what worked for us: a combination of relaxing sounds, a simple log to understand sleep cycles, and routines that provide security to the baby.

Thank you for letting us be part of your nights!
